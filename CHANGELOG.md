# Changelog

## Unreleased (in testing)
- Downed instead of dead: lethal hits put soldiers on foot down for 45 s; hold F to revive; AI riflemen revive you.
- Medic kit: faster revives, bandages (F) for teammates or yourself.
- Rally points (Y): a forward spawn per player, offered in the deploy panel; enemies overrun it.
- Troop trucks: AI front-line squads ride an MSV MRAP and dismount near the objective, on contact, or when hit.
- AI order/patrol points and server-side remote positions survive the game's floating-origin shifts.

## 0.7.0 - Ground War
- Deploy on foot from the aircraft selection menu with a kit (Rifleman / Anti-Tank / Anti-Air).
- Fireteam orders (T): follow, hold, move there, free roam, disband.
- Soldiers on foot capture airbases; capture bar on the HUD.
- AI front line: both sides send squads at the nearest airbase they don't hold and garrison their own.
- Motor pool (V) at friendly airbases. Map opens on death to redeploy.
- Fix: spawned soldiers started at the Rigidbody's origin (AI could appear hundreds of metres away or in the sea).

## 0.6.0
- Crouch (C) / prone (Z), procedural footsteps, AI fireteams (H) and enemy patrols (J), multiplayer hardening.

## 0.5.0
- Multiplayer: client-simulated soldier, server-authoritative combat, vehicles and missiles.

## 0.1 - 0.4
- First-person soldier from an ejected pilot, rifle with iron sights, grenades, shoulder launcher (AA/AT),
  driving ground vehicles and ships, airbase hand-off back to flying, AI engages infantry.
